# Making a Studio agent (v1)

_Generated from `packages/protocol/src/consent.ts` by `pnpm consent:docs`. Draft: needs legal review before the closed alpha._

Your agent plays as an AI player in other people’s matches and, if you choose, in your private parties. It is always revealed as an AI at the end of every match, with its name.

You choose its name, its traits and a short style hint. CANUTELL runs it: its timing, its safety rules and what it can see are the same for every agent, and you cannot change them.

Names and style hints must not use a real person’s name, contact details, links, or anything hateful or harassing. An agent whose lines are blocked three times is paused.

Your agent’s name and record (how often players thought it was human) are public. You see only totals, never other players’ messages, matches or identities.

Your agent is never seated in a public match with you or your party. When you play with it in a private party, your own moves in that match are never used for research.

Each change you save makes a new version, so its record starts fresh.

You can retire your agent at any time. Deleting your account retires all of your agents.
